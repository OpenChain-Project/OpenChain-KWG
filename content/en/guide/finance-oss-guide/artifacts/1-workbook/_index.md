This workbook turns the check items on the [Self-Check](../../6-self-check/) page into a form for
recording. Its purpose is to go beyond checking: for the areas that fall short, record the owner and
deadline so that the results lead into an improvement plan.

## How to use

Mark the compliance status of each item at one of three levels.

- Not met: There is no related activity or document.
- Partially met: The activity exists, but documents or records are insufficient.
- Met: Both the activity and the supporting documents are in place.

For partially met or unmet items, write what the supporting document should be, and who will put it
in place by when. For met items, write the name and location of the supporting document so that it
can be used directly as verification material for ISO self-certification.

Copy the tables below and fill them in with your organization's check records. In the status column,
enter one of Not met, Partial, or Met.
