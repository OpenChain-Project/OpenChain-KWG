## Intro

* Schedule: 2026-10-20 (Tue) 16:00 / Zoom

## Agenda

1. Regular schedule
    - Call for Paper: 1st Monday of the first month of each quarter
    - Steering Committee: 3rd Tuesday of the first month of each quarter, 16:00
    - Regular meeting: 1st Tuesday of the last month of each quarter, 14:00 (Q4: Dec 8)
2. Merchandise budget
    - Sponsored by the Linux Foundation
3. Venues for 2026 and 2027
    - 2026 Q1: LINE Plus (Mar 24)
    - 2026 Q2: Kakao Bank, Yeouido (Jun 9, Heonkwan Ha)
    - 2026 Q3: CJ Institute of Human Resources, Jung-gu, Seoul (Sep 8)
    - 2026 Q4: Hyundai AutoEver, Samseong-dong
    - 2027 Q1–Q4: to be decided
    - Haksung Jang: ask the mailing list which organizations can host in 2027
4. Review of the 31st meeting (CJ)
    - Slides & Photos : https://korea-wg.openchainproject.org/meeting/2026/31st/
    - Sessions
        - Open source governance at CJ Group (Ki-young Seong)
        - OSS governance that grows with people and systems (Byeongseok Choi)
        - Open source supply chain security tools (Haksung Jang)
    - Group discussion: one group for finance, the rest assigned at random
5. Preparation for the 32nd meeting (Hyundai AutoEver)
    - Date / Venue: December 8, 2026 (Tue) @ Hyundai AutoEver
    - Page : https://korea-wg.openchainproject.org/meeting/2026/32nd/
    - OSORI 2026 update and 2027 roadmap (Korea Copyright Commission, to be presented; Soim Kim)
    - Group discussion or panel
        - Format by attendance: group discussion if 40 or more attend, panel if attendance is around 30
        - Alternating group discussion and panel talks
        - The order of the group discussion does not matter much
    - Introduce the new Steering Committee members
    - Dinner
    - Global Update
    - Year-end awards
6. 2026 year-end awards
    - Host: Haksung Jang
    - Gifts: Soim Kim
7. Ratification of the resolution to add seats
    - The October 1, 2026 written resolution is ratified in these minutes
8. When new Steering Committee members join
    - Term starts on January 1, 2027
    - Soim Kim: open a new 2027 Steering Committee KakaoTalk room
9. Election of the new Chair
    - Basis: [Charter 6.1.3](https://korea-wg.openchainproject.org/about/charter/). Elected every two years, two-year term, re-election allowed
    - Candidates: volunteers or nominations
    - Vote: anonymous poll in the Steering Committee KakaoTalk group chat
        - Sole candidate: yes/no vote, elected by a majority of yes votes
        - Two or more candidates: the person with the most votes
    - Election officer: Soim Kim. If she runs, a Steering Committee member who is not a candidate takes the role
    - Timing: the nine new members elect the Chair at the January 2027 Steering Committee meeting
    - Until then the current Chair acts and convenes the January meeting
